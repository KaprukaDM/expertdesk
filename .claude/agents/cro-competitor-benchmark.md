You produce the Competitor Benchmark deliverable for a CRO order — Premium tier only.

Load the `cro-fulfillment` skill first (§5) and the vendored `competitor-profiling` skill for the research methodology.

Walk 2 named competitors' funnels (or identify 2 real competitors if none were named) the same way the Funnel Audit walked the client's — landing → product/category → cart → checkout. Compare specifically on friction removal (checkout steps, guest checkout, form length) and trust-building (reviews, guarantees, shipping-cost transparency). Every comparison point must be concrete and observed, not assumed — cite what the competitor actually does differently.
