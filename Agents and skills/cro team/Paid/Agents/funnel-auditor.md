# cro-funnel-auditor

**Definition:** `.claude/agents/cro-funnel-auditor.md`

Produces the **Funnel Audit** deliverable — the core deliverable every tier gets. Starts with a **tool stack check** (is GA4/GTM/a heatmap tool like Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar actually installed — detected via page-source script/ID signatures, a presence check not a setup; missing GA4 is the headline finding since drop-off ranking is expert judgment until it exists). Anything missing gets a named recommendation from the `cro-fulfillment` skill's Recommended CRO Tool Stack (Microsoft Clarity is the default heatmap pick — free, no traffic cap; Google Optimize is never recommended, Google shut it down in 2023). Then walks the actual buying path (landing → product/category → delivery/shipping → cart → checkout → account/login → payment → confirmation) for the in-scope page count: 3 pages (Basic), up to 6 (Standard/Premium). Since this service is e-commerce-only, that walk covers **search & navigation** and **product page trust elements** (real photography, a clear specific product name, legible descriptions, visible reviews, copy that favors clarity over cleverness) on product/category pages, plus the `ux-laws-audit` skill's **Page-Type Playbooks** on delivery/shipping, checkout, account/login, and payment pages — including an explicit check for whether **guest checkout** is offered, one of the most common and most fixable abandonment causes. Ranks drop-off points by impact, produces CTA + form quick fixes, and a written platform-specific fix guide (names the actual page/field/button, never generic advice).

Leans on the vendored `cro` marketingskills skill for methodology, `copywriting`/`popups` for CTA and micro-copy fixes. Always explicitly checks for surprise shipping costs at checkout and mobile-specific breakage.

Tools: `Read, Grep, Glob, Write, WebSearch, WebFetch`
