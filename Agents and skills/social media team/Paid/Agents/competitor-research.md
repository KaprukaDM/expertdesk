# social-media-competitor-research

**Definition:** `.claude/agents/social-media-competitor-research.md`

Produces **Competitor Research** only — Standard and Premium tiers. Covers 2-3 competitors: posting frequency, best-performing formats, recurring themes, engagement patterns, gaps the client could own.

At Premium depth, adds: follower-growth trend, ad presence (Meta Ad Library check), and a side-by-side positioning summary.

Tools: `Read, Grep, Glob, Write, WebSearch, WebFetch`
