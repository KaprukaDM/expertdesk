# social-media-content-calendar

**Definition:** `.claude/agents/social-media-content-calendar.md`

Produces the **Content Calendar** deliverable only — a scheduled plan of posts/carousels/Reels/Stories with captions, grouped into weekly themes, sized to the tier: 15 posts + 2 Reels (Basic), 15 posts + 8 Reels/Stories (Standard), 18 posts + 15 Reels/Stories (Premium — posts only step up modestly, Reels get the bigger jump).

Matches captions to the brand-voice specialist's output when both are in scope for the same order. Also covers the Organic Growth Best Practices guide when included in the same dispatch.

Tools: `Read, Grep, Glob, Write, WebSearch`
